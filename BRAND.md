# Brand and Naming Standards

These rules are binding for all copy, metadata, and structured data in this
repository, and for any client facing document produced under this brand. When
in doubt, follow this file over anything in existing copy.

## Brand name

The client facing brand name is "Hollinger AI" only.

Do not use "Power Solutions", "Power Systems", "Solutions", or any other suffix.
Do not attach a tagline. "Applied AI Performance Systems" is retired and must not
appear in copy, titles, alt text, keywords, or schema fields.

Use "Hollinger AI" in page titles, footers, image alt text, Open Graph and
Twitter siteName, and the Schema.org organization name. Do not set an
alternateName that repeats or extends the brand.

## Legal entity

The legal parent entity is "Hollinger Holdings Corporation". Hollinger AI is a
division of Hollinger Holdings Corporation, not a subsidiary. Never write
"Hollinger Holdings Ltd" or "subsidiary".

On the website, "Hollinger Holdings Corporation" is the correct full reference in
schema and structured data. The shorthand "A Hollinger Holdings company" is
acceptable as a footer line. Keep capitalization consistent: lowercase "company".

Invoices are the sole exception. Invoices use "Hollinger Holdings Corporation dba
Hollinger AI" as the billing entity for GST and bookkeeping, per the bookkeeper.
Never mix entity names within a single document.

## GST and HST

The GST and HST number for all invoices is 760596130 RT0001. It belongs on
invoices only, never on marketing pages.

## Language

Strip sales language. Do not use "cutting edge", "unlock", "transform",
"transformation", "bespoke", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy", "world class",
or superlatives about our own work. State what the system does and what it costs.

Describe future phases as honest possibilities, never as commitments.

## Domains

The website domain is hollingerai.online. The holding company domain is
hollinger-holdings.com. These are URLs and are exempt from punctuation rules.
