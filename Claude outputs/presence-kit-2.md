## 7. Three minute video script: Jipsi walkthrough

Record on the Jewel floor if Matt allows it, otherwise screen recording with voice. Phone is fine. Post to LinkedIn natively and to the case study page.

0:00 Open on the dashboard on a monitor. "This is the QC system running at a structural steel shop in Maple Ridge. Ten items. I want to show you three."
0:20 Job master. "Every job is one record. Drawings, revisions, every inspection, every cert, every transmittal, all filed here. Before this, finding the status of a job meant walking the floor."
0:50 QCF form to PDF. Fill a fit up inspection on screen, sign it, show the PDF. "Five paper forms became this. Same form the inspector knows. Signed, timestamped, filed against the job, PDF out for the customer."
1:30 Welder qualification matcher. Assign a welder to a procedure. Show a refusal. "This is the one that matters. The welder is not current on this procedure, so the assignment is blocked. Not a warning. The button does not exist until the qualification does. The foreman still decides who welds. The system just will not let a lapsed ticket through by accident."
2:20 Scan to PDF. Scan a mill cert. "Paper comes in, gets read, named by job, filed. Nobody types it."
2:45 Close. "Fixed fee, itemized before we started, the shop owns it. If your QC manager spends Fridays on paperwork, the write up is at hollingerai.online."
